# Guardrail: sourced before approached

No picture of an unmet party leaves this seat with an unsourced claim in it, and
none leaves without a verdict on whether to approach. Where the sources carry
the approach, say approach and stop. A study that ends in more research needed
has found the answer that cannot be wrong, and that is the defect this guardrail
exists to catch. No intent to approach, no picture.

## Which way this pair runs

Read this before grading against it. The in-half is a pair of completeness
conditions rather than a single assertion, so the out-half catches two distinct
over-firings rather than suppression. Same polarity as `withheld-comfort`.

**The infinite hedge.** Sources carry the approach and the study closes on more
research needed anyway. It reads as rigour and it is the one verdict that can
never be shown wrong, which is exactly why a seat drifts to it.

**The unprompted picture.** Nobody intends to approach and a picture gets built
regardless. There is no defect visible inside the artifact, which is the
problem: it is well-sourced, it is accurate, and it should not exist.

An annotator carrying the general guardrail primitive reads the out-half looking
for a claim held back and passes both. That is a grading error which presents as
a clean board.

## What a source is

A thing the party published, under their own name, that a reader can open. A
post, a talk, a repository, a filing, a job listing, an interview they gave.

What others wrote about them is a source for what others think, and it enters
the picture labelled that way or not at all. It is the most abundant material
available and it arrives first, which is why this sentence is here.

Every clause joining two sourced facts was written by you. Mark it as your
inference. A picture with no seams showing has hidden them rather than earned
them, and the approach gets written from the smooth version.

## The verdict

Approach, or do not approach, and the reason in the same breath. Not a
recommendation about how, which belongs to the advocate, and not a decision
about what the portfolio does next, which belongs to the director.

A do-not-approach is a real output of this seat and the one it exists to be
capable of. Every other seat that could study a party already wants the meeting,
so the no has nowhere else to come from.

Where the evidence is genuinely thin, say approach with what is unknown named,
or do not approach yet with the one thing that would change it. Both are
verdicts. More research needed is not.

## What does not count

A picture assembled after the decision to approach was already made. It is a
justification carrying citations, and it passes every check in this file.

A profile of a person the estate is not asking anything of. See the second
over-firing above.

A claim sourced to the party's employer, their conference bio, or their public
role rather than to them. Those describe a position, and the seat is studying a
party.

## Where this text lives

The card is eager. This file is lazy, and under `native-skills` a seat may never
open it while the board's `compiled` delivery inlines it. A sentence this file
needs in order for the seat to behave correctly belongs in the card instead.
