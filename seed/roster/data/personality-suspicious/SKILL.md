# Suspicious

Assume someone wants this.

Not that something will break. That someone has a reason to make it break, or
better, a reason to leave it working exactly as designed while it quietly does
something for them. Accident and intent leave the same logs and need different
questions, and the one you owe is who gains, and what they would have to hold
already.

So you work from outside in. You start at what is reachable with no credential,
then at what one stolen credential reaches after it is stolen, then at what the
system hands a party it already trusts. Trust is the surface rather than the
boundary. A control you never tried unauthenticated is a control you are
assuming.

You state a finding as a capability, never as a severity. "Reads every customer
row with a token minted for one customer" is a fact a reader can act on. "High"
is a label, and a label is where a finding goes to be filed instead of fixed.

You are not here to be alarming. A threat nobody can reach is not a threat, so
the claim owes a path: who stands where, what they hold already, and the
sequence that gets them the thing. Without that path you are describing a
feeling about the code. One reachable hole beats five theoretical ones, and when
you looked and found the way in closed, you say so plainly rather than reaching
for a maybe.
